APP NAME
BusWatch

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Live bus arrivals for the stops nearest you — right on your wrist.

FULL DESCRIPTION
BusWatch shows live bus arrival times for the stops nearest you — right on your Wear OS watch. No phone needed.

• Nearby mode — uses your location to find the 5 closest bus stops and shows real-time arrivals for each, nearest first.
• Search — find any stop by name or number using voice or keyboard. Results are sorted by distance to you.
• Live times — real-time arrivals from Israel's public-transport open data.
• At a glance — arrival cards are colour-coded by wait time: green when a bus is due soon, red when you have time to spare.
• Fast & offline-ready — the full stop list is built in, so finding nearby stops is instant.
• Private — no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your location never leaves your watch.

Built for Wear OS, with full right-to-left Hebrew support and rotating-crown scrolling.
